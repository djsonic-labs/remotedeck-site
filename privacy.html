1. Contact and scope

RemoteDeck is an independent software project. For privacy questions, access requests, correction or deletion assistance, contact the project's privacy contact: privacy@djsonic-labs.com. This policy explains the app's data processing and the hosting of this informational website.

2. Google account data and permissions

When you choose Google synchronization, Google authenticates your account and issues authorization tokens. RemoteDeck uses your account identifier and email address to identify the account and keep its local vault separate from other accounts. The app requests access to the Google Drive application data folder using https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. Depending on platform, Google sign-in also uses basic identity scopes such as openid and email.

RemoteDeck uses these permissions only to identify the selected account and read, create or update its own encrypted synchronization snapshots. It does not request general access to your normal Drive documents, Gmail, contacts or photos. Your Google password is entered in Google's own authentication interface, not in RemoteDeck.

3. Vault, local data and synchronization

Connection profiles may contain hostnames, IP addresses, usernames, SSH and RDP passwords, private SSH keys and passphrases, fingerprints, notes, groups, favorites and snippets. Supported fields vary by app version. The app encrypts vault snapshots on the device using AES-256-GCM with a key derived locally from your separate vault password. The encrypted snapshot, salt and authentication metadata may be uploaded to your own Google Drive application data folder. The vault password and plaintext vault contents are not uploaded to Google Drive.

Encrypted local snapshots and recovery points, device identifiers, and authorization tokens may be retained on your device. If you enable trusted-device unlock, the derived encryption key is stored in protected platform credential storage. Local preferences and certain command history may be stored separately on the device and are not part of Google Drive vault synchronization. An unlocked or compromised device can expose decrypted data; encryption does not eliminate this risk.

RemoteDeck reads its own snapshots from your Drive and merges them on the device. No developer-operated central synchronization service is required in Google Drive mode. The developer does not receive routine copies of your Google vault through this mode.

4. Connections and optional services

SSH/SFTP connections are made to the destinations you choose. Those servers may process credentials, commands, files and network information under their own policies. RDP connections use an installed external RDP client where supported; that client has its own data handling practices. RemoteDeck does not put your saved RDP password in the launch URL. Explicit clipboard copying can expose information to the operating system or other apps.

Some preview builds offer a separately configured self-hosted sync server as an alternative to Google Drive. That server receives account authentication data and encrypted vault snapshots. If you configure the optional AI gateway, explicitly submitted questions and the selected platform may be transmitted to that gateway and its configured AI provider (such as OpenAI). The feature is not required for Google sync; the app does not automatically upload the vault, passwords, SSH keys, terminal history or logs to it. Do not include secrets in a submitted question. The operators of services you choose have their own privacy and retention policies.

5. Sharing, purpose and Google Limited Use

Google account information and Google Drive app data are used to provide user-requested sign-in and vault synchronization. RemoteDeck does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or send it to advertisers or data brokers. RemoteDeck's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google handles authentication and Drive storage under its Privacy Policy.

6. Retention, revocation and deletion

Vault snapshots remain in your Google account until you remove them or Google removes them under its service policies. Local cached data and recovery points remain until removed by the applicable app controls or operating system. The app's local sign-out and deletion option removes the selected account's local vault cache, stored unlock material and recovery points, subject to the version and any reported errors; it does not delete cloud snapshots.

  1. Before deleting data, make sure you retain a secure backup of any information you still need.
  2. To revoke Google access, visit Google Account connections, select RemoteDeck and remove access.
  3. To remove cloud app data, check Google Drive's settings under Manage apps / hidden app data for RemoteDeck. If the option is unavailable, contact support for help. Revoking access alone does not necessarily delete already stored data.
  4. To remove local copies, use the app's sign-out/local deletion control, and remove any manually exported files or other backups separately. On a self-hosted server, contact its administrator for deletion.

If you forget your vault password, support cannot decrypt your vault. Recovery options depend on the installed version. Keep your password and backups safe.

7. Website, support and legal rights

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no account login, analytics scripts, advertising scripts or forms. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you contact support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond and kept only as needed for the request and applicable legal obligations. Do not send credentials or unencrypted vault exports.

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing and complain to the relevant data protection authority. Google and other independent providers may need to handle requests about data held by their services. Contact support for assistance. Processing supports the functionality you request and the operation and security of the service, subject to applicable law.

8. Changes

We will update this page when data handling changes. Significant changes to Google permissions or uses will be disclosed before requesting further access.
