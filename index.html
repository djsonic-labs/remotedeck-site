RemoteDeck is a remote connection manager for Android and Windows. Organize SSH and RDP hosts, work in your terminal, transfer files over SFTP and synchronize your encrypted vault across devices.

Built for remote administration

SSH & SFTP

Manage hosts, open terminal sessions and transfer files directly between your device and your servers.

Remote Desktop

Keep Windows connection profiles separate from SSH. RDP preview builds launch a compatible external client.

Encrypted sync

Sign in with Google and use your own Google Drive app-data storage. Your separate vault password protects your saved credentials.

Organize your work

Groups, favorites and reusable command snippets keep frequently used connections close at hand.

Private preview

RemoteDeck is under active development. Access to preview builds is currently limited to invited testers. The public website does not distribute the private application or provide a browser login.

RemoteDeck is an independent project. Google Drive is used for optional synchronization; Google is not the developer of RemoteDeck. The website requires no Google permissions.
