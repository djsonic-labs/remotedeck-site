RemoteDeck support

Getting started

Invited testers can install the signed Android APK or extract the portable Windows x64 ZIP supplied by the developer. Version 0.5.1 has passed automated build checks; real-device sign-in and sync testing is in progress. Choose Google Drive, sign in with your Google account, and unlock the vault with your existing vault password or set one up when creating a new vault. Some Windows preview versions still require the developer's desktop OAuth configuration.

Restore connections on another device

Use the same Google account and vault password. Wait for synchronization to finish and check the displayed sync status. If a vault is missing, stop and contact support rather than deleting or reinitializing data.

Switch accounts safely

Use a separate test account for a new vault. Keep an existing vault backup before upgrading. If an account unexpectedly appears new, do not overwrite or reset your vault; contact support first. Google sign-in and the separate vault password serve different purposes.

Contact

For access to the preview, sign-in problems, sync issues, or security reports, write to support@djsonic-labs.com. For privacy requests, contact privacy@djsonic-labs.com.

Include your app version, platform and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, private keys, Google access tokens, unencrypted vaults or confidential server information.

To revoke Google access or remove your data, follow the instructions in our privacy policy.
